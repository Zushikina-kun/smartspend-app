CORE CONCEPT
------------
AI-assisted mobile financial tracking and advisory app for Filipino users.
Pipeline: User Input → AI Parsing → SQLite DB → Analytics & Insights
The AI is the primary entry point for all data input.
Architecture: Agentic AI with dynamic context injection (NOT RAG).
