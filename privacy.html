Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026 · SmartSpend v2.9.88 · Lucid Frame
SmartSpend is a capstone research project developed at Lorma Colleges by Lucid Frame. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

1. Data We Collect

SmartSpend collects the following data when you use the app:

  • Account information — Email address, display name, and profile photo (optional) when you create an account via Firebase Authentication.
  • Financial data — Expense records, income entries, budget settings, savings goals, debt records, and wallet balances that you enter manually, by voice, or via AI chat.
  • Usage data — AI message count (for daily rate limiting), app settings, and feature usage patterns stored locally on your device.
  • Receipt images — Photos you take or import for OCR scanning. These are processed locally or sent to our AI provider (Google Gemini / Groq) for text extraction and are not stored on any server.
  • Crash reports — Anonymized crash logs sent to Firebase Crashlytics to help us fix bugs.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide core app features — expense tracking, budgeting, financial health scoring, and AI financial guidance.
  • To sync your data across devices via Firebase Firestore (if you are signed in).
  • To generate AI-powered financial insights using your expense history as context for the AI model. Your data is sent to third-party AI APIs (Google Gemini, Groq) only when you actively use the AI chat feature.
  • To improve app stability and fix crashes via Crashlytics.

3. Data Storage

  • On-device — All financial data is stored in a local SQLite database on your device. It is accessible only by the SmartSpend app.
  • Cloud sync — If you sign in, your data is synced to Firebase Firestore under your user ID. Only you can access your data — Firestore security rules enforce this.
  • No third-party advertising — We do not share your data with any advertising network.
  • No data sale — We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

4. AI and Third-Party Services

When you use the AI chat feature, your message and a summary of your financial data (recent expenses, budget totals, goals) are sent to one of the following AI providers:

  • Google Gemini (generativelanguage.googleapis.com) — Primary
  • Groq Cloud (api.groq.com) — Fallback
  • Cerebras Cloud (api.cerebras.ai) — Last-resort fallback

These services have their own privacy policies. The data sent includes only financial summaries — not your name, email, or any identifying information beyond what is needed to answer your question. API keys are stored in Firebase Remote Config and are not embedded in the app binary.

5. Permissions

  • Camera — For scanning receipts and barcodes. Not used for anything else.
  • Microphone — For voice expense input. Audio is processed locally by the device's speech recognition.
  • Internet — For Firebase sync, AI chat, and exchange rate fetching.
  • Storage / Photos — For saving receipt photos and reading imported images.

SmartSpend does not request access to SMS, contacts, call logs, or location.

6. PII Protection (RA 10173 — Data Privacy Act of 2012)

In compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), SmartSpend takes the following measures:

  • Mobile phone numbers are stripped from text before it is sent to AI providers.
  • You can delete all your data at any time through Profile → Delete Account.
  • Cloud data is scoped strictly to your Firebase user ID — no other user can access it.

7. Financial Advice Disclaimer (RA 11765)

In compliance with the Financial Products and Services Consumer Protection Act (RA 11765):

  • SmartSpend AI provides general financial guidance based on your logged data — not professional financial advice.
  • SmartSpend is not a licensed financial adviser, bank, or financial institution.
  • For major financial decisions (loans, investments, insurance), consult a licensed professional.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. You can:

  • Export all your data at any time via Profile → Export Backup (JSON) or Transactions → Export CSV.
  • Delete all local data via Profile → Settings → Delete All Data (this clears the local SQLite database).
  • Delete your account and all associated cloud data via Profile → Delete Account.

9. Children's Privacy

SmartSpend is intended for users 13 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy as the app evolves. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent version. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
