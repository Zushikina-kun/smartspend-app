What is SmartSpend?

SmartSpend is a free, AI-powered personal finance app built specifically for Filipino spending habits — students, young professionals, and anyone trying to take control of their money without a finance degree.

Most finance apps are built for Western users — they don't understand GCash, Paluwagan, ShopeePay Later, Pag-IBIG, or the reality of living on a daily allowance. SmartSpend does.

You can talk to Peso — the AI — in Taglish. Log "nag-gastos ako ng 65 sa kainan" and it logs the expense, picks the category, and deducts it from your wallet. No forms, no friction.

👩‍🎓 Students 💼 Young Professionals 🏠 Household Managers 🔧 Freelancers

How it works
From download to daily use in 3 steps.
1
📲
Download & Set Up

Install the APK, choose your account type (Student, Employed, Freelancer, etc.), set your daily allowance or income, and add your wallets (Cash, GCash, bank). Takes 2 minutes. No account required.

2
💬
Log Your Expenses

Just tell Peso what you spent — in Filipino, English, or Taglish. Or snap a photo of your receipt. Or paste your GCash transaction history. SmartSpend handles the rest: category, wallet deduction, tags.

📊
Understand Your Money

Check your Financial Health Score, see where your money goes, set savings goals, and ask Peso for personalized advice. "Saan ako nag-o-overspend?" — Peso knows, because it's read your actual data.

🚀 On the Roadmap

The Next Step: AI That Lives on Your Phone

On-device LLM inference on Android has crossed a meaningful threshold in 2026. Flagship phones with Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 and Tensor G6 chips deliver 45–60 TOPS of NPU performance — enough to run 1B–4B parameter models at 20–50 tokens per second, faster than most people read.

What this means for SmartSpend:
We're actively researching full on-device AI for a future release. When stable enough for financial use, Peso would run entirely on your phone — no PC, no WiFi, no server.

Candidate models we're evaluating:

Gemma 3 1B/4B Phi-4 Mini (3.8B) Qwen 3 1.7B
  • Gemma 3 1B/4B (Google) — runs at 30–40 tok/s on mid-range devices, multilingual including Filipino
  • Phi-4 Mini (Microsoft, 3.8B) — ~3 GB at Q4 quantization, strong reasoning for its size
  • Qwen 3 1.7B — fast, compact, good at structured financial tasks

What we're waiting for:
The bottleneck isn't hardware — it's reliability. Financial AI needs to be accurate on peso amounts, dates, and categories every time, not 90% of the time. We're monitoring llama.cpp, MediaPipe, and MLC-LLM maturation on Android before committing to a release date.

What it would mean for you:
Zero cloud dependency. Zero API costs. Peso answers your budget questions even on airplane mode. Your entire financial life — conversations, analysis, advice — processed entirely on the glass in your pocket.

* On-device AI availability depends on device hardware and model stability. Mid-range devices (6GB+ RAM) are the target minimum.

Yes. All core features — logging expenses, viewing analytics, managing budgets, tracking goals, checking your FHS — work fully offline. Internet is only needed for Peso AI chat responses and the live currency/market data feed. If you use Local AI mode (connecting to your own PC), even AI chat is offline.

Your expense data lives on your device — not on our servers. When you use AI chat, the conversation text (not your full expense history) is sent to an AI provider to generate a response. If you use Local AI mode, nothing leaves your home network at all. We don't sell data, don't run ads, and don't have a business model that depends on your information.

Android 5.0 (API 21) or higher — which covers virtually every Android phone made after 2014. Install the arm64-v8a APK for any phone from 2018 onwards (this is 99% of phones in use today). If that doesn't install, try armeabi-v7a.

Not yet — SmartSpend is Android-only for now. An iOS version is something we'd love to build, but as a student capstone project with limited resources, we focused on Android first (where most Filipino users are). The app is built with Flutter, so an iOS port is technically feasible in a future version.

FHS is a 0–100 score calculated from 4 pillars: Savings Rate (are you saving ≥20% of income?), Budget Adherence (are you staying within your category budgets?), Budget Setup (have you configured budgets?), and Logging Consistency (are you tracking regularly?). Each pillar contributes 25 points. The score updates in real time as you log expenses.

Local AI mode lets Peso run on a local LLM server on your own PC instead of sending messages to cloud providers. You need to install Ollama, LM Studio, or Jan on a PC connected to the same WiFi network as your phone. Once set up, go to App Settings → AI Model → Local AI, enter your PC's IP address, and Peso runs entirely on your hardware. This is optional — the app works great with the default cloud AI too.

Yes. You can talk to Peso in Filipino, English, or Taglish — mix them freely. "Nag-gastos ako ng 65 sa Jollibee" works the same as "I spent 65 at Jollibee". Peso parses amounts, merchants, categories, and payment methods from natural language, including common Filipino shorthand (like "fare" for jeepney fare, "load" for prepaid data/calls).

Yes, in several ways: paste the transaction SMS/text from GCash, BPI, BDO, or Maya directly into Peso chat and it extracts the expenses automatically.
