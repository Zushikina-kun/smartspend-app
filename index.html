Everything you need to manage money
Built specifically for Filipino spending habits, income cycles, and financial products.
🏆
Financial Health Score
A real-time 0–100 score based on your savings rate, budget adherence, debt load, and spending control. Dual-mode: full or lightweight.
🧾
OCR Receipt Scanner
Point your camera at any receipt or screenshot — SmartSpend reads it and logs all items automatically. Supports 40+ platforms including GCash, Shopee, Lazada.
🎯
Savings Goals
Set goals with deadlines and track progress. Auto-calculate monthly contributions. Peso celebrates every milestone from 25% to 100%.
💚
Safe to Spend
Know exactly how much you can freely spend before your next payday after reserving upcoming bills, goal contributions, and debt payments.
💳
Installment Plans
Track ShopeePayLater, GCash GLoan, HomeCredit and any fixed monthly payment. See remaining balance, due dates, and total interest.
🏦
PH Government Contributions
Calculate exact SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG contributions based on your income. BIR TRAIN Law tax estimates included.
📱
Use Without an Account
Tap "Continue Without Account" to start immediately — no sign-up, no email. Your data lives on your device. Connect a free account anytime when you're ready to sync.
🔒
Local AI — Private Mode
Run the AI on your own PC with Ollama, LM Studio, or Jan. Your financial data never leaves your home network. Enable "local-only mode" so it never falls back to cloud if your server is unreachable.
💬
Chat Sessions & History
AI conversations are saved as sessions. Start a new chat anytime — your old session is archived. Browse, rename, search, or delete sessions from Chat History.
🔍
Sort, Group & Filter
Sort transactions by date, logged date, amount, name, or source. Group by category (with subtotals), date, or source. Collapsible group headers. Search with relevance ranking.
🔔
Recurring Transactions
Auto-detect subscriptions (Netflix, Spotify, GCash) and recurring bills. One-tap to log all due bills. Semester interval for students.
🏵️
Paluwagan Tracker
Track your rotating savings group — who paid, whose turn is next, total pot, and payout schedule. Uniquely Filipino.
📊
Analytics & Charts
Spending by category (pie), monthly trend (bar), savings rate vs 20% target (line), FHS history, mood-spending correlation, and more.

🤖 Meet Peso — Your AI Finance Coach

Ask Peso anything about your finances in Filipino or English. It reads your actual data before answering — not generic advice.

You Nag-gastos ako ng 250 sa jollibee kanina gamit GCash → Logged + wallet deducted
You How am I doing this month? Pwede pa ba akong gumasta? → Safe-to-Spend analysis
You Split the bill 3 ways for 1,500 dinner → Splits + logs ₱500 expense
Peso Based on your 6-month trend, your Food spending is 23% above average this month. You still have ₱1,240 safe to spend until payday on Oct 30.

8-provider fallback chain: Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite → Flash → GPT-OSS 120B → Qwen3.6 → Qwen3.8 → Compound → Compound Mini → Cerebras — always finds a working model.

About the Project

SmartSpend is a capstone thesis project developed by Lucid Frame at Lorma Colleges, College of Computer Studies and Engineering (CCSE), Bachelor of Science in Information Technology — 4th Year, Academic Year 2026–2027.

Technical Highlights

  • Flutter / Dart — Android (API 21–36)
  • SQLite local database (v14, 26 tables)
  • Firebase Auth, Firestore, Remote Config, Crashlytics
  • Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite as primary AI (AQ. key via Remote Config)
  • 9-provider AI fallback chain — never fails
  • Fully offline — all core features work without internet
  • Open source — MIT Non-Commercial License
