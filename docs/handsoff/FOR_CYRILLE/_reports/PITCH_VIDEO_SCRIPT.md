## 🎯 KEY MESSAGING PILLARS

### 1. **Problem-Solution Fit**
- Problem: Manual tracking abandoned, overspending unnoticed
- Solution: Automated multimodal entry + proactive AI guidance

### 2. **Filipino-First Design**
- Taglish support (not just English)
- GCash integration (93.3% of users have it)
- Local financial workflows (cash + e-wallet)

### 3. **AI That Actually Helps**
- Not just charts — actual conversations
- Proactive warnings before overspending
- Context-aware advice based on YOUR spending patterns

### 4. **Privacy & Offline**
- 100% works without internet
- Local AI processing
- Your data stays on YOUR phone

### 5. **Proven & Tested**
- Real user testing (30 participants)
- Academic research backing
- Measurable accuracy (91% OCR, 94.6% categorization)
