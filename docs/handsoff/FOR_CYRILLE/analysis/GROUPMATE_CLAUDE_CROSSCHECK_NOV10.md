### 5.2 Bangor SUS Adjective Anchors

**Claim**: "OK and Good anchors" (v2 Slide 14, MANUAL_EDIT_GUIDE A16, A27)

**Research Finding**:
[Bangor, Kortum, and Miller (2009) developed an adjective rating scale](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/228368593_Determining_What_Individual_SUS_Scores_Mean_Adding_an_Adjective_Rating_Scale) to help interpret SUS scores. Their study correlated SUS scores with adjective ratings. A score around 68.5 was rated as "OK", while 76.6 was rated as "Good". The adjective scale includes levels such as Worst Imaginable, Poor, OK, Good, Excellent, and Best Imaginable. Content was rephrased for compliance with licensing restrictions.

**Citation**: Bangor, A., Kortum, P. T., & Miller, J. T. (2009). Determining what individual SUS scores mean: Adding an adjective rating scale. *Journal of Usability Studies, 4*(3), 114–123.

**Verdict**: ✅ **CORRECT** — A score of 61.83 falls between "OK" (≈50-70) and "Good" (≈71-80), as v2 states. This is more accurate than calling it simply "OK" or "Good".

### 5.4 BSP Circular 1133 Scope

**Claim**: Circular 1133 applies to lending/financing companies and OLPs, not banks (MANUAL_EDIT_GUIDE A22)

**Research Finding**:
[BSP Circular No. 1133 (Series of 2021)](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Regulations/Issuances/2021/1133.pdf) prescribes ceilings on interest rates and fees charged by Lending Companies (LCs), Financing Companies (FCs), and their Online Lending Platforms (OLPs). The circular became effective in early 2022 and targets unsecured, short-term loans up to ₱10,000 with tenure up to four months. Content was rephrased for compliance with licensing restrictions.

**Scope**: LCs, FCs, and their OLPs — NOT banks.
