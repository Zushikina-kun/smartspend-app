## 🎯 What to say at the defense

1. **What the app does:** SmartSpend is an offline-first Android app that lets Filipino users log expenses in Taglish, import GCash screenshots automatically, and see a real-time 0–100 Financial Health Score — no bank account needed.
2. **Survey results:** N=30 respondents — 96.7% would use an AI financial app, 93.3% use GCash already. These numbers validate every feature we built.
3. **SUS score:** Our usability score is 61.83 out of 100 — rated "OK" by Bangor et al. (2009). It's below the 68-point average, and we acknowledge that honestly. It's a prototype-stage result and we documented it as a limitation.
4. **FHS formula:** The Financial Health Score is not made up — we based it on the CBA–Melbourne Institute Observed Financial Health Indicator (Comerton-Forde et al., 2018). Four equal components, 25 points each.
5. **Not for rich people only:** The app is free, works offline, runs on ₱3,000–₱5,000 Android phones, and GCash users have zero manual logging. The people who need this most are the ones who can't afford financial mistakes.
