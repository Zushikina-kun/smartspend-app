Sum of Contributions: 30
SUS Score Formula:
SUS Score = Sum of Contributions × 2.5
SUS Score = 30 × 2.5 = 75.0
Final SUS Score
75.0
Adjective Rating: "Good" (Bangor et al., 2009)
Note: This example shows a sample respondent scoring. For odd-numbered items (1, 3, 5, 7, 9), the contribution is the scale position minus 1. For even-numbered items (2, 4, 6, 8, 10), the contribution is 5 minus the scale position. The sum is then multiplied by 2.5 to obtain the overall SUS score, which ranges from 0 to 100.
Reference: Brooke, J. (1996). SUS: A "quick and dirty" usability scale. In P. W. Jordan et al. (Eds.), Usability Evaluation in Industry (pp. 189–194). Taylor & Francis.
