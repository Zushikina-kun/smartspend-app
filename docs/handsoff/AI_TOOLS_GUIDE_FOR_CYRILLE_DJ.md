## TOOL 2 — ChatGPT / Claude — 6 Research Prompts

Use these prompts to improve the manuscript. **Always paste your actual text** — never let it write from scratch without your content.

### Prompt 1 — Research Strategy
> "Act as an expert academic researcher. Analyze my capstone study about SmartSpend — an AI-assisted personal finance app for Filipino parents and young professionals. The app uses a custom Financial Health Score (FHS) adapted from the CBA-Melbourne Institute framework. Identify any research gaps I haven't addressed, suggest stronger research questions, and check if my 3 objectives are measurable."

### Prompt 2 — Literature Review Improvement
> "Review this paragraph from my literature review: [PASTE PARAGRAPH]. Compare it to the existing studies I've cited. Identify: (1) what's well-supported, (2) what claims need citations, (3) what conflicting viewpoints I haven't addressed, (4) what research gaps I should highlight. Use only verifiable academic sources."

### Prompt 3 — Research Methodology Check
> "I used a mixed-methods descriptive-developmental design with purposive sampling (N=30: 20 parents 35-55, 10 young professionals 21-35) and SUS evaluation. Review this methodology. Explain whether the design is appropriate for my 3 objectives, what limitations I should acknowledge, and whether my sampling strategy is defensible for a capstone prototype."

### Prompt 4 — Chapter Writing
> "Create a 200-word paragraph for my Chapter III (Results) about the FHS formula design. Write in formal academic English. The paragraph should: (1) state that the FHS was designed as a Prototype Observed Financial Health Indicator, (2) explain the equal-weight 4-component design, (3) cite Warren & Tyagi (2005) for the 20% savings target, (4) cite Kahneman & Tversky (1979) for the Overspend Control loss-aversion design, (5) acknowledge it is a prototype pending empirical validation."

### Prompt 5 — Critical Analysis (run this on your chapters)
> "Review this section of my capstone manuscript like a strict academic adviser: [PASTE SECTION]. Identify: (1) claims without citations, (2) weak or circular reasoning, (3) inconsistencies with my stated objectives, (4) methodological issues, (5) anything a panel would question. Give specific improvements for each issue."

### Prompt 6 — Final Thesis Review
> "Review this section for: clarity (is it easy to understand?), structure (does it flow logically?), grammar and word choice, APA 7th edition citation format, and consistency with academic writing standards. Flag: unverifiable claims, unsupported statistics, and any sentence that a panel could challenge. [PASTE SECTION]"
