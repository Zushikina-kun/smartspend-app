    1. What is this app and why does it matter?
    What problem does SmartSpend solve?

    Most Filipinos don't track their finances because it's tedious — manually opening an app, filling forms, picking categories, remembering amounts. SmartSpend removes that friction: you just talk to it naturally ("I spent 60 for lunch and 30 for jeep") and it logs both expenses automatically. For Filipino students and young professionals already glued to their phones, this lowers the barrier to building a financial habit.

    Who is your target user?

    Two groups: (1) Filipino college students with irregular allowances, handled via the allowance cycle and student account type. (2) Young working professionals aged 21–35 who earn a salary but have never tracked finances before. Designed mobile-first, AI-assisted, to fit existing phone habits.

    How is this different from BudgetPH or PISO?

    1) AI with 34 agentic actions — the others have no AI, you log manually; we support voice, OCR, and screenshot import. 2) Batch screenshot import from 40+ platforms (Shopee, GCash, Steam, bank apps) — unique to SmartSpend. 3) Financial Health Score — a single 0–100 score across 4 dimensions; others just show balance and history.

    2. How does the Financial Health Score work?
    Explain the FHS formula simply

    4 equal parts, 25 points each, total 100:

    Savings Rate (25): full points at 20% savings, scales proportionally below. 25 × min(1, actual_rate / 0.20)

    Overspend Control (25): 25 × (days_within_budget / days_elapsed)

    Budget Adherence (25): 25 × (categories_on_track / total_budgeted). No budgets set → full 25, we don't penalize new users.

    Logging Consistency (25): 25 × (days_logged / days_elapsed_this_month)

    Clamped 0–100. Above 80 is excellent.

    What if someone has no income, like a student?

    Two modes. Full Mode uses all 4 components including Savings Rate, which needs income data. Lightweight Mode (students can enable) swaps income-dependent components for spending-habit ones: Spending Restraint, Logging Consistency, Category Balance, Habit Streak — a meaningful score with no salary needed.

    Why 20% savings target specifically?

    From the 50/30/20 budgeting framework — 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings — widely recognized in personal finance literature and cited in BSP financial literacy recommendations. Used as benchmark so users get a clear, researched goal instead of an arbitrary number.

    How does the score handle logging gaps?

    Logging Gap Detection: when days show no log but wallet balance changed (suggesting spending happened), the app asks "Did you have transactions on those days?" Confirming yes applies a small penalty to Logging Consistency; confirming a genuine no-spend day gives a small bonus. More accurate and more honest than silently ignoring missing days.

    3. How does the AI work?
    Is the AI safe? Does it access our financial data?

    The AI receives only typed text and a summarized expense context — categories and amounts, no names or personal identifiers. Mobile numbers are redacted before sending. Expense data lives locally in SQLite and never leaves the phone except for encrypted Firebase sync. Compliant with RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act).

    What happens when the AI is down or internet is slow?

    An 8-provider fallback chain: Gemini → Groq models → Cerebras, each with its own daily limit. The app tracks and persists the active provider across restarts. If all 8 fail or hit limits, manual entry — log, view, analyze — still works 100% offline.

    How do you prevent the AI from making mistakes?

    Three layers: (1) Auto-categorization evidence threshold — 10 prior "Food" logs can't be flipped to "Others" on one uncertain response. (2) Action allowlist — import/OCR flows can only trigger expense-logging actions, never delete or modify account settings. (3) Confidence score 0–1 per AI-logged expense; below 70% shows a "Review" badge.

    Who pays for the AI?

    Free tiers across providers — Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite (primary) free via Google AI Studio, Groq and Cerebras free API access. 150 messages/day per user across all providers, at zero cost for a research project. Production scaling would move to paid tiers via Firebase Remote Config, which is already implemented for key management.

    4. Technical implementation
    Why Flutter and not native Android or React Native?

    Single codebase targeting Android, iOS, and web. For our Android-only capstone scope it also gave first-party ML Kit access for OCR and barcode scanning. Dart iterates faster than Kotlin for UI work — mattered given a 1-semester timeline.

    How does batch screenshot import work?

    User selects multiple gallery screenshots. Each runs through Google ML Kit text recognition on-device (no internet needed). Extracted text goes to the AI with a structured prompt to extract item, amount, date, merchant. The AI returns JSON, parsed and shown for user confirmation before saving. 40+ platforms supported via keyword detection for their receipt formats.

    How is data stored? Is it secure?

    Primary storage: SQLite on-device (20 tables), synced to Firebase Firestore for cloud backup. API keys are not stored in the APK — fetched from Firebase Remote Config at startup. Firebase App Check blocks unauthorized API access. Local security: PIN or biometric lock.

    Database schema version and table count?

    Schema version 11, 20 tables — expenses, income, budgets, wallets, savings_goals, debts, recurring, installments, insurance_policies, paluwagan, chat_history, score_history among them. Migrations handled in the onUpgrade callback so existing user data is preserved.

    5. Research and methodology
    What is your research methodology?

    Agile Kanban — Backlog → In Progress → Done in sprints. Evaluation: System Usability Scale (SUS), 10-item questionnaire, 0–100 score, 30 respondents (20 parents, 10 young professionals) post-defense. Targeting above 80, mapping to "Good" on the Bangor et al. (2009) adjective scale.

    What makes this a capstone contribution?

    (1) Dual-mode FHS — adapts its formula to whether the user tracks income; no other Filipino finance app has a similar system. (2) Multi-modal AI input with 34 agentic actions in Taglish — voice, OCR, barcode, screenshot import in one app. (3) 8-provider AI fallback chain — a resilience architecture for AI availability across free-tier providers, practical for apps that can't afford paid subscriptions.

    How is this different from just using ChatGPT?

    ChatGPT has no persistent data — context is lost each session. SmartSpend maintains a complete on-device financial database (218 expenses, 5 wallets, 3 savings goals in our test account) and executes 34 action types: saves to the database, updates wallet balances, sends notifications, calculates scores. ChatGPT Finance is also US-only and requires a bank link via Plaid — unavailable in the Philippines.

    6. Hard questions / challenges
    What are the limitations of your app?

    (1) The AI works best with clear input — ambiguous messages can produce wrong categories; mitigated by the evidence threshold but not perfect. (2) No Play Store release yet — requires Google's 14-day closed testing with 12 testers. (3) No iOS version — Flutter supports it but needs a Mac with Xcode. (4) The FHS formula isn't validated with real user data yet — the SUS survey and post-defense testing will address that.

    How do you handle RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act)?

    Four measures: (1) PII redaction before sending to AI — mobile numbers and email patterns stripped. (2) One-time AI advice disclaimer clarifying we're not a licensed financial adviser (RA 11765 compliance). (3) Data stored locally by default; Firestore sync is user-controlled. (4) Privacy policy discloses what's collected, how it's stored, and user rights — to be hosted as a Play Store submission prerequisite.

    What happens after the 150 message/day AI limit?

    A clear message that the daily limit is reached, resetting at midnight UTC. Manual entry stays fully functional — Log Expense, Edit, Budgets, Analytics, all Hub tools work without AI. Limit is per user, per day; at 150 across 8 providers, total capacity is ~1,200/day across all users — sufficient for a research prototype. Production scaling would use paid API tiers.
