## Full Description

**SmartSpend: The First AI-Powered Finance Tracker Built for Filipinos 🇵🇭**

Tired of budgeting apps that don't understand your lifestyle? SmartSpend speaks your language — literally. Track your spending in Taglish or English, and get personalized AI advice that actually makes sense for Filipino finances.

**🏛️ FIRST IN THE PHILIPPINES: Government Contributions Tracker**
SmartSpend is the only personal finance app that helps you track mandatory contributions:
✅ SSS (Social Security System) contributions with pension estimator
✅ PhilHealth premium tracking
✅ Pag-IBIG contributions and MP2 savings
✅ Direct links to official government portals
✅ Year-to-date totals and historical tracking

Perfect for freelancers, employees, OFWs, and anyone managing government mandatories.

**💬 NATURAL LANGUAGE EXPENSE LOGGING**
Just talk or type naturally:
• "₱150 pamasahe kanina sa jeep"
• "Jollibee breakfast ₱85"
• "Monthly GCash GLoan payment"

Peso (our AI mascot) understands you and logs everything automatically.

**📸 7 WAYS TO TRACK EXPENSES**
1. Voice input (Taglish/English)
2. Take photo of receipt (OCR)
3. Scan barcode
4. Manual entry
5. Batch import screenshots (GCash, Maya, Shopee, etc.)
6. Paste bank CSV
7. AI chat (natural conversation)

**🧠 FINANCIAL HEALTH SCORE (0-100)**
Get real-time insights into your financial wellness across 4 pillars:
• Savings Rate
• Overspend Control
• Budget Adherence
• Logging Consistency

Know exactly where you stand and what to improve.

**💚 "SAFE TO SPEND" AMOUNT**
SmartSpend calculates how much you can freely spend before your next payday — after bills, goals, and necessities. No more anxiety about whether you can afford that coffee.

**🇵🇭 BUILT FOR FILIPINO FINANCES**
✅ GCash & Maya transaction import
✅ Paluwagan (rotating savings group) tracker
✅ PH Banks comparison guide (20 banks + digital banks)
✅ Installment plans (ShopeePayLater, GLoan, HomeCredit)
✅ Semi-monthly payday cycles (15th & 30th)
✅ 57 currencies with live exchange rates

**🏆 GAMIFICATION & MOTIVATION**
• 36 Achievement Badges
• Daily Quests
• Expense logging streaks
• Peso mascot celebrations
• Daily mood check-ins with spending insights

**🤖 MEETS PESO — YOUR AI MONEY COACH**
Peso is a friendly ₱ peso coin character who:
• Guides you through the app
• Celebrates your wins
• Gives gentle nudges when needed
• Speaks warm, non-judgmental Taglish
• Shows 12 different moods based on your finances

**🔒 PRIVACY-FIRST DESIGN**
• Use without account — start immediately
• Data stays on your device
• Local AI mode option (zero cloud)
• Optional Firebase sync across devices
• RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act) compliant
• App lock with PIN + biometric

**📊 POWERFUL ANALYTICS**
• Spending trends & pie charts
• 50/30/20 rule breakdown
• FHS history tracking
• Budget vs actual comparison
• Savings rate projections
• 3/6/12 month forecasts
• AI-powered financial advice

**💡 FILIPINO AI THAT UNDERSTANDS YOU**
Our AI knows about:
• Jeepney fare, siomai, taho
• GCash, Maya, Coins.ph
• 7-Eleven, Jollibee, SM
• 11.11, 12.12 sales
• Pasko bonus, 13th month pay
• Utang, paluwagan, pautang

**💳 COMPREHENSIVE DEBT & SAVINGS TRACKING**
• I Owe / Owed to Me tabs
• Savings goals with milestones
• Emergency fund calculator
• Payment plan tracking
• Peso Cost Averaging calculator (MP2, UITFs, stocks)

**🌟 WHY SMARTSPEND?**
✨ FREE to use, no hidden fees
✨ No financial institution access required
✨ Works 100% offline (except AI features)
✨ Multi-provider AI with 9-model fallback chain
✨ Regular updates from active dev team
✨ Research-backed (Lorma Colleges capstone project)

**📱 SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS**
• Android 7.0 (Nougat) or higher
• 120 MB storage space
• Internet for AI features (optional Local AI mode available)

**🎓 ACADEMIC RESEARCH PROJECT**
SmartSpend was developed as a capstone research project at Lorma Colleges (AY 2026-2027) by the Lucid Frame team under the guidance of Dr. Janelli M. Raposas. The app has been formally evaluated with a System Usability Scale (SUS) score and is continuously improved based on user feedback.

**📢 YOUR FEEDBACK MATTERS**
Use the in-app feedback feature (shake your device) to report bugs or suggest features. We actively listen and improve!

**Download SmartSpend today and take control of your finances — the Filipino way! 🇵🇭💚**
