Chapter II · Theoretical Framework

Built on Behavioral Economics

🔔 Nudge Theory

Thaler & Sunstein (2008)

Choice architecture guides behavior without restricting freedom. Budget alerts, Warning Decay, and startup gap prompts are designed nudges.

💡 Self-Determination Theory

Deci & Ryan (2000)

36 badges + streaks = Competence; customizable goals = Autonomy; Filipino-first framing = Relatedness.

📉 Prospect Theory

Kahneman & Tversky (1979)

Losses feel ~2× more painful than gains. Warning Decay: FHS drops −5 pts/day when budget warnings are ignored.

🏦 FHS Academic Basis — CBA–Melbourne Institute (2018)

The Financial Health Score adapts the CBA–Melbourne Institute Observed Financial Health Indicator (Comerton-Forde et al., 2018) — a transaction-based 0–100 scale with equal-weighted components. We call ours a Prototype Observed Financial Health Indicator (FHI) per Melbourne Institute naming guidance. UNSGSA (2021) validates using transaction data as an early-stage complement to survey-based scales.

Chapter II–III · Financial Health Score

The FHS Formula — Where It Came From

FHS (Full Mode) = 4 × 25 pts each: ① Savings Rate = 25 × min(1, rate/0.20) ② Overspend Ctrl = 25 × (1 − weighted/active) ③ Budget Adher. = 25 × (onBudget/total) ④ Log Consistency = 25 × (loggedDays/active) Adjustment: Warning Decay −5 pts/day (max −15) Lightweight Mode: Spending Restraint + Category Balance + Logging Consistency + Habit Streak

Chapter III · Data Privacy

Privacy-First AI — RA 10173 Alignment

☁️ Cloud AI (Default)

Requests go through 9-provider chain. Before sending, our 5-pattern on-device PII redaction layer removes:

• Philippine mobile numbers (09XX/+63)
• GCash/bank reference numbers (12–18 digits)
• OTPs and PIN codes
• Bank account numbers
• IBAN / full account strings

Note: Free-tier Gemini content may be used to improve Google products.

🏠 Local AI (Private)

User connects Ollama, LM Studio, or Jan on their own PC. While server is reachable, no data leaves the home network.

Local-only toggle: When ON, AI stops responding instead of falling back to cloud — for maximum privacy.

📱 On-Device AI (Roadmap)

Gemma 4 E2B (INT4, ~2.6 GB) via LiteRT LM SDK. Viable on 6 GB RAM phones on Android 10+.

Current status: Planned for v5.0 (~2027) when 6 GB phones are majority of PH market.

Listed as Recommendation, not delivered feature.

📦 What is sent to cloud AI per request (~5,000–8,000 tokens)

Recent 10 expenses (item names + amounts only) · Budget limits · Savings goals · Wallet balances · Conversation history · Philippine financial reference data (SSS/PhilHealth rates, BNPL providers) · No account numbers, no receipt images, no personal IDs

Chapter IV · Scope Boundaries

Limitations & Scope Boundaries

📉 SUS Below Benchmark

Final SUS N=30 (mean 61.83, SD = 17.58) is below the 68-point average benchmark and below the pre-established target of ≥80. The result reflects the prototype stage of the system. Identified UI friction points are documented in recommendations.

🕐 No Longitudinal Study

Evaluation is cross-sectional SUS only. No behavior change was measured. The FHS decay, Peso mascot, and badge system are designed interventions — their effect on actual spending habits is unvalidated.

🏦 No Direct Bank API

SmartSpend relies on screenshot import and manual entry. Direct bank-credential integration was excluded due to the absence of accessible open-banking APIs in the Philippine market and RA 10173 concerns.

📶 Offline AI Limitations

All AI chat features require internet connectivity (except Local AI mode). Core tracking functions work fully offline, but Taglish expense parsing and AI advisory are cloud-dependent.

🧮 FHS is a Prototype Indicator

The 0–100 FHS is a Prototype Observed FHI — not a validated psychometric instrument. It has not been cross-validated against a standardized financial health survey (e.g., CFPB Financial Well-Being Scale).

📱 Android-Only Scope

Current build targets Android API 21+. iOS was excluded from the evaluation scope. Purposive sampling from La Union means results are not nationally generalizable. OCR/categorization figures are internal estimates.
